Meet Your Walker

Hi, I'm Brandon, Chief Woof Officer here at Bone Cadets. I've walked dogs professionally for over a decade, first starting in Santa Monica before moving to San Francisco seven years ago for school. For the past three years, I've been back doing what I love: full-time dog walking in SF. I've worked with every type of dog you can imagine: energetic puppies, wise seniors, couch potatoes, and absolute athletes. Every dog has their own personality, quirks, and ways of showing what makes them happy. Nothing beats discovering those things with each one.

I started Bone Cadets with a simple mission: To turn every walk into an adventure. Whether your dog needs one-on-one focus or thrives in a carefully matched pack, my goal is to give them their best day ever, capture moments in photos you'll want to show off, and help build and reinforce skills, confidence and good behavior along the way.
