Meet Your Walker

Meet Your Walker

I founded Bone Cadets because over the past 13 years of walking and caring for dogs, I've learned that nothing beats giving dogs their best days ever. From energetic puppies to wise old souls, each breed and temperament has taught me something about what makes tails wag the hardest.

Operating out of San Francisco, Bone Cadets isn't just about walks. Whether solo, in a pack or at the park, Bone Cadets' mission is to turn every outing into an adventure where dogs build skills, confidence, and pure happiness.

-Brandon, Chief Woof Officer
